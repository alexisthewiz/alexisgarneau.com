Fascinated by the journey of building. Currently investing in startups and partnering with outlier founders at Pathlight Ventures.

I'm drawn to startups for 2 main reasons: 1) startups as a means of expression, 2) startups as a means of progress.

  1. I believe most of our time on earth is meant for self-discovery. A core pillar of discovering the self is expression (i.e., throwing something into the world), which I find to be one of the purest joys of the human experience. Expression can unfold through several mediums, most notably art. I believe startups to be a means of expression. Like a painting, a startup is an artifact (a living one) deeply tinted by the personality of its founder(s). And a startup is a world of its own, one with its own philosophy, quirks, brand, and feel.
  2. Startups do not simply pop out of nowhere. Someone, somewhere, has decided to apply an opposing (often irrational) force to the world's status quo, because they saw an inefficiency that could be solved for. That makes us all better off. Most of these bursts of irrationality blatantly fail. But when the ingenuity, energy, and time of a founder hit a pain point right on the forehead, they gain economic value and can be traded for money (sometimes lots of it), and that is a beautiful thing.
